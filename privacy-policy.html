Fairwright: Transit Tycoon: privacy policy

OurCraftBaby · last updated 4 November 2026

Fairwright: Transit Tycoon ("the game") is made by OurCraftBaby. This policy explains what the game collects, why, and the choices you have.

In short

  • The game plays offline and needs no account.
  • It collects nothing about you unless you say yes to sharing crash reports and play data, or choose to send us feedback, and you can change that at any time.
  • We never collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos or messages, and we never sell data or show ads.

Crash reports and play data (only if you agree)

The first time you play, the game asks whether to share crash reports and anonymous play data. If you say yes, it sends us:

  • Crash reports: what went wrong, where in the game, the game version and phone model.
  • Play data: events such as finishing a tutorial step, building a first line, the tools used, how long a session lasts, company value each game year, frame rate, graphics setting and the phone's heat status.
  • An app-instance ID: a random number created by Firebase that links these events together. It is not linked to your name or Google account.

We use this only to fix crashes and to improve and balance the game. It is processed by Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config) on our behalf and sent over an encrypted connection. Google's handling is described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

You can turn sharing off at any time in Menu › Settings › Share play data. When it is off, nothing is sent.

Game settings

Settings such as graphics, sound and language, and your saved games, are kept only on your phone.

Feedback

If you use Menu › Send feedback, the game sends us a report with:

  • your message;
  • the game version, phone model, Android version, memory, screen size and graphics setting;
  • the game's date, map size and seed, and the game settings in use, so we can replay what you saw;
  • the play-data ID (only if you share play data), so we can match the report to a crash;
  • if you choose, a copy of your game and its play log (what you did in the game recently and the game's own error messages, nothing from outside the game).

Nothing is sent unless you tap Send. The report goes over an encrypted connection to a private channel on Discord that only OurCraftBaby can read; Discord stores it on our behalf (see discord.com/privacy). If you are offline, the report waits on your phone and is sent the next time the game starts; a report not sent within 30 days is deleted. In builds without this, the game opens Android's share sheet instead and you choose how to send it, usually by email.

We use feedback only to answer you and to fix and improve the game, and delete it when it is no longer needed, at the latest 12 months after it arrives. The report does not include your name or email; if you want a reply, write your email in the message. To have a report deleted, email us with the date you sent it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Keeping and deleting data

Play data is kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days, then deleted. To have the data linked to your app-instance ID deleted sooner, email us with the ID shown in Menu › Settings › Play data ID and we will do so.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU or UK under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data and to object to its use. Email us to use these rights. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
