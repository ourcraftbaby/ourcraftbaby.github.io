Fairwright: Transit Tycoon

Build the routes. Grow the towns.

A transport tycoon made for your phone. It starts in 1930, with a little money and a map of sleepy towns, and the roads and railways you build carry them into the future.

Join the betaAndroid · closed beta now

What you do

Roads and rail

Drag to build roads, track, bridges and tunnels. Plan bus, truck and train lines with a few taps.

Freight chains

Coal, grain, wood, steel, food and goods: feed the factories and the towns that need them thrive.

Steam gives way to diesel and electric

Towns change their look as the decades pass, a few buildings at a time. From 1930 to 2050 and beyond, on the same map you started with.

Towns that grow

Serve a town well and it grows, from village to city to metropolis, street by street.

Made for touch

Designed for phones from the start, with a guide and goals that teach you as you play.

Lights on after dark

By the 2040s the towers glow at night, their windows and trims lit along the coast.

Yours to keep playing

No account to create and nothing to sign in to. It plays offline, anywhere.

No ads, ever

In English and Español.
